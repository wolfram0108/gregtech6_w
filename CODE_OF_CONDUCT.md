# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Argue about code, not about people. This project deals with a large, old, intricate
codebase where being wrong is routine — including for maintainers — so treat a mistake as a normal
part of the work rather than as something to mock.

## Expected behaviour

- Keep criticism about the change: what it does, what it breaks, what evidence supports it.
- Accept that "I don't know" and "I was wrong" are good answers, and do not punish people for them.
- Assume the other person is trying to help until shown otherwise.
- Respect the project's rules even when you disagree with them; argue for a change, then abide by
  the outcome.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Personal attacks, insults, or demeaning comments.
- Harassment of any kind, in issues, pull requests or any other project space.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing other people's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption: repeatedly reopening settled decisions, bad-faith argument, deliberate noise.

## Scope

This applies to everything in the project's spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit
messages, release notes — and to public behaviour that identifiably represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through GitHub's
[private reporting](https://github.com/wolfram0108/gregtech6_w/security/advisories/new), which is not public. Reports are handled discreetly; the
reporter's identity is not disclosed.

Maintainers may remove or edit contributions, and may block accounts, when behaviour violates this
document. Responses are proportionate: a word first, removal or a block if it continues.

This document is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
shortened to what this project actually needs.
